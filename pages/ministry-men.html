The Men's Ministry

The Men’s Ministry is committed to building godly men who are rooted in Christ and equipped to lead in their families, church and communities. The ministry encourages men to grow spiritually, take responsibility and live out their faith with integrity and purpose.
Through fellowship, teaching and mentorship, the Men’s Ministry supports men in strengthening their relationship with God and becoming positive role models.

What We Do:

  • Men’s fellowship meetings
  • Bible study and prayer sessions
  • Annual Men’s Day celebrations
  • Community outreach and service projects
  • Workshops on leadership, family, and personal development

Who can join: men of all ages.
